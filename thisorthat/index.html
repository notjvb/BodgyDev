ThisOrThat

A collaborative decision matrix for iPhone. Pit two options against each other, add what matters, and let everyone pull each criterion toward the one that wins it.

iPhone · iOS 18+No accountsSibling of ProsNCons

For "this one or that one?"

ProsNCons is for yes-or-no questions. ThisOrThat is for the other kind: Civic or RAV4, Lisbon or Kyoto, the blue couch or the green one.

  • Two options, shared criteria. Each decision has a "this" on the left and a "that" on the right, each in its own color.
  • Everyone votes, the weight is shared. Each criterion has a weight anyone can change, and each person leans it up to three notches toward either option. Other people's votes ride above the slider as little avatars.
  • Everyone, or just me. Flip the scoreboard between the group's verdict and your own.

How the score works

⚖️Start at even

"This" starts with 50 points. A criterion nobody has voted on counts as even, so it can't tip anything.

🏋️Weigh what matters

Every criterion's average lean is multiplied by its weight, from "A little" to "Top priority".

💯Scaled to 100

A unanimous "way better" on every criterion is a perfect 100. "What's driving it" lists the criteria moving the score most.

🔁Swap sides

Votes belong to a side, not a name. Rename an option and the votes stay. Swap sides and every vote flips with it.
