Bell Bearing

A compass that always points at the nearest Taco Bell, dressed up as a sci-fi motion tracker. Its return blip pings higher the closer you get.

iPhone · iOS 17+No accounts, no API keysSynthesized sound

Somewhere out there, a Crunchwrap.

Bell Bearing does one job. Open it, and an arrow points at the nearest Taco Bell, with the range ticking down as you walk. No map, no list, no reviews: just a bearing and a distance, the way a motion tracker would give them to you.

  • Tap the blip to lock onto that location instead of the nearest one, and tap again to let go.
  • NAV hands off to Apple Maps for directions. SCAN forces a fresh search.
  • PHOS cycles the phosphor: P1 green, P3 amber, P56 red.
  • Tap the range to switch between metric and imperial.

Built like a prop

Half app, half movie prop. Every part of the tube is drawn live.

📺A real-ish CRT

A Metal shader bends the picture across a curved tube, with scanlines, color fringing, grain, flicker, the occasional glitch, and a power-on warm-up.

🔊Every sound synthesized

The pulse and the return ping are generated on the fly, with haptics to match. There isn't a single sound file in the app.

🧭Search in rings

Places come from Apple's MapKit search, in widening rings until something turns up. No API keys and no server of its own.

🔩Wear and tear

The keys sit on a physical chin with indicator LEDs, scuffed by procedurally generated grime and scratches. A self test runs at boot.

Bell Bearing is a fan project. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, Taco Bell. It just really likes them.
