How things get made here

A few habits every app on the bench shares.

📱

Native, all the way

SwiftUI and Swift 6 on the phone. No web views pretending to be apps.

🪶

Light on the backend

Small, boring servers that cost next to nothing to run and nothing to babysit.

🦦

Ask for as little as possible

No sign-up where an app doesn't need one. A random otter is a fine identity.

🍬

Playful on purpose

Chunky buttons that press down, springy motion, and colors you could eat.
