ProsNCons

A collaborative pros & cons list for iPhone. Start a decision, list the good and the not-so-good, and text a link so friends can weigh in.

iPhone · iOS 18+No accountsUpdates in seconds

Decide out loud, with your people.

Some decisions are better made with a group chat's worth of opinions. ProsNCons gives everyone the same two columns and lets them fill them in together, from wherever they are.

  • Owners own the framing. Only a decision's creator can change its title, notes and color, or invite and remove people.
  • Everyone owns the list. Anyone in a decision can add, edit, move or delete pros and cons, and everyone sees it within a few seconds.
  • Invites are one-time links. Each link works for exactly one person and expires after a week. Text it, and it opens straight into the app.

The little things

🦦No accounts

You get a random identity, like "Bubbly Otter", the first time the app opens. It rides along in iCloud Keychain to your other devices. Tap it to shuffle.

🔔Polite notifications

When someone joins or changes a decision, everyone else gets one short summary, like "🦊 Zippy Fox added 2 pros", at most once every few minutes.

🪶Built to run for free

The whole backend fits on Cloudflare's free tier. An open decision checks for news every few seconds for the cost of reading a single row.

🗑️Leave anytime

Delete your data from the app and you're gone, along with the decisions you started. Tokens and invite codes are only ever stored as hashes.
